How to read channel metrics

Subscriber counts indicate audience size; public view counts show observed activity. A useful comparison also considers topic fit, video age, format, typical views, and whether a small channel has an unusually successful video. These public signals cannot reveal a channel's private impressions, click-through rate, or retention.

CREATOR PRINCIPLES

There is no magic formula.

YouTube recommendations are personalized. Focus on the viewer, make a clear promise, deliver value early, and use your channel's own analytics to learn. Successful channels are examples to study—not templates to copy.

  • Appeal: does the topic, title, and thumbnail make the value clear?
  • Engagement: does the video deliver on its promise and keep viewers interested?
  • Satisfaction: do viewers find the experience useful and worth their time?
