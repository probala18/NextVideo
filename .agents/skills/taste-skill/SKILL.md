# tasteskill v2 - Anti-Slop Frontend Skill

A page built by a model usually looks like the average of every page the model has seen: a centred
headline over three lines, a little label above it, a row of icon cards, a soft gradient, a few thin
rules and a monospace caption. Nothing in it is wrong and nothing in it was decided.

This skill replaces those defaults with decisions:

1. **Anti-Gradient & Real Materiality:** No fuzzy purple/cyan gradients. Use deliberate, physical contrast, deep solid backgrounds, precise borders, and honest color accents.
2. **The idea comes from the subject:** YouTube creators need clear, actionable signals (CTR vs baseline, retention drops, scene-by-scene script timings, video opportunities, and studio-grade tools).
3. **Typography & Rhythm:** High contrast, deliberate typography hierarchy. Large, confident headlines, micro-labels with purpose, and readable data metrics.
4. **Motion with Intent:** Spring physics with Framer Motion and cinematic precision with GSAP. Motion must feel physical, responsive, and purposeful.
5. **No AI Slop Tells:** No centered 3-line generic text, no floating random emojis, no useless card repetition. Every component has a dedicated utility, interaction, or data flow.
